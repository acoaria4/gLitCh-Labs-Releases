Your data controls

AURA’s Privacy panel gives you controls over saved information and optional network features. These instructions follow the existing Privacy Policy.

Export and manage saved information

In the app’s Privacy panel, you can export local or account-associated data available through the API, clear the prediction cache, and delete saved horoscope profiles.

Choose optional network features

You can enable or disable online geocoding and tradition corpus citations in Privacy settings. With online geocoding disabled, location search uses the offline place database.

Request help with deletion

Uninstalling the mobile app does not necessarily remove profiles or caches stored on the hosted service. Use the in-app Privacy controls, or contact us to request deletion. Tell us which installation or profile the request concerns; we can help identify the information needed.

Email a data request ↗

Send private requests by email rather than posting birth details in a public issue.
