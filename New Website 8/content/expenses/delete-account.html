Delete your Expenses account

Expenses by gLitCh Labs
App ID: com.glitchlabs.expenses
Last updated: 8 August 2026

If you created an Expenses account by signing in (for example with Google), you can request that we delete that account and the cloud data tied to it. You do not need the app installed to make this request.

How to request deletion

  1. Email glitchlabsio@gmail.com with the subject line Expenses account deletion request.
  2. Send the message from the same email address you used to sign in (or clearly state that Google/Apple account email in the body).
  3. Optionally include your display name in Expenses and that the app is Expenses by gLitCh Labs com.glitchlabs.expenses.

Email deletion request

We will process requests within a reasonable time and confirm by email when the account and associated cloud data have been deleted.

What we delete

When we complete your request, we delete cloud data associated with your account, including:

  • Account / profile records (including display name and phone on your profile)
  • Synced personal vault data (expenses, activity, categories, people, settings, reminders, etc.)
  • Your membership in shared cloud groups and related cloud ledger data owned through that membership

What may be retained

  • Data other members still need in a shared group (for example an expense you logged that remains part of the group’s history) may be kept in anonymized or member-orphaned form where required for the group to stay consistent — we do not keep your sign-in identity for that purpose.
  • Limited records we must keep for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance, if any, only for as long as required.
  • Data that exists only on your device (local-only use, or after sign-out) is not on our servers. Clear it with Settings → Reset in the app, or by uninstalling / clearing app storage.

Sign out without deleting

In the app, Settings → sign out disconnects cloud sync on that device but does not delete your cloud account. Use the email steps above to delete the account.
