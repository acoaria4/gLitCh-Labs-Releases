About

The language of calm.

gLitCh Labs builds focused, privacy-minded software. Every product should feel quiet, inevitable, and at home in someone’s day — never loud for its own sake.

Codex

Six pillars

Philosophical foundations — not decoration — for how our products should feel.

Silence

Silence creates space for thought.

Not emptiness — the deliberate removal of distraction so people can focus on what matters.

Clarity

Clarity removes the need for explanation.

Interfaces should communicate themselves. You should sense what something is, where it belongs, and when you need it.

Balance

Nothing exists at the expense of something else.

Beauty should never reduce usability. Functionality should never diminish elegance. Every decision seeks equilibrium.

Restraint

Nothing exists until it earns its place.

Every feature, colour, animation, and component must justify itself. If it doesn’t improve understanding, confidence, or delight — it doesn’t belong.

Calm

Nothing should compete for the user’s attention.

Attention is precious. Interfaces should guide it intentionally rather than constantly demand it. Calm creates confidence.

Belonging

Software should feel familiar long before it feels learned.

People should feel at home. Information belongs where it’s expected. Products adapt to people — not the other way around.

Inevitability

When those pillars coexist, the interface disappears. The highest compliment a gLitCh Labs product can receive is: “Of course that’s how it should work.”
