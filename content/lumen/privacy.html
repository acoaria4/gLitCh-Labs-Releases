Privacy Policy

Lumen by gLitCh Labs
App ID: com.glitchlabs.lumen
Last updated: 6 November 2026

Lumen is a quiz-based learning app for Grades 9–12. This policy explains local practice, sign-in, ranked Daily Spark, optional cloud backups, subscriptions and support. Unranked Daily Spark does not require an account. Online features depend on availability in your installed version.

1. Who we are

Lumen is published by gLitCh Labs. We use information to save learning progress, authenticate your account, operate rankings, provide backups you request, verify paid access, prevent misuse and respond to support requests. For privacy questions or a data request, email glitchlabsio@gmail.com.

2. Your local learning profile

Lumen stores your nickname, chosen grade, theme, daily goal, onboarding preferences and learning progress on your device. Progress includes quiz answers and history, XP, streaks and Daily Spark completion. Native apps use device preferences; the browser build uses local browser storage.

In versions offering a custom picture, the picture you choose is cropped and resized on your device. It is not sent to the leaderboard, Firebase Authentication or RevenueCat, and is excluded from Lumen's platform cloud-backup file. It is separate from the preset leaderboard avatar and profile information supplied by Google or Apple during sign-in.

Change your profile in Settings → Profile. Settings → Data and privacy → Reset app clears the device's learning profile and local recovery copy. Clearing app or browser storage can also remove local data. Signing in or restoring a purchase does not itself back up or recover all learning progress; use the separate backup controls below. Downloaded ranked attempts, queued answers and cached rankings can also be saved locally for offline completion and later upload.

3. Google and Apple sign-in

Lumen uses Firebase Authentication for Google and Apple sign-in. Firebase assigns an account identifier (UID) and processes provider identifiers and available account information, such as your email address, display name and profile-photo URL. Apple may supply a private relay email address. The information supplied depends on your provider and choices.

This information authenticates your account and connects it to subscription access and, where connected, ranked play. The ranking service receives a Firebase sign-in token to verify requests. Authentication and hosting services also process connection and security information, including IP addresses and user-agent information, to operate their services and prevent abuse. Lumen does not receive your Google or Apple password.

See Firebase’s privacy and security information, Google’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

4. Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Apple's App Store or Google Play processes Lumen Pro subscription payments. Available plans may be monthly or yearly. Lumen uses RevenueCat to verify purchases, restore purchases and determine Pro access. Lumen sends your Firebase UID as the RevenueCat app user identifier so purchases can be associated with your account.

RevenueCat processes purchase and transaction information, product identifiers, subscription status and expiry, and technical app/device information needed to provide its service. Its dashboard also uses purchase information for subscription reporting and analytics. Lumen does not send your quiz history, nickname or chosen local picture to RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service for subscription access.

5. Ranked Daily Spark and the leaderboard

When you are signed in and rankings are connected, Lumen sends your nickname, selected preset avatar, grade and streak information to the ranking service to create or update your learner profile. Ranked play also sends attempt identifiers, selected answer identifiers and completed results. The service records dates, answer acceptance times, correctness and scores to calculate weekly rankings, enforce daily-attempt limits and investigate conflicting answer submissions.

Your leaderboard nickname, preset avatar, score and rank can be shown to other signed-in learners. Use a nickname rather than your full name, email address, school or contact details. Your sign-in email and custom local picture are not included in leaderboard rows. Unranked practice history is not uploaded to the ranking service.

The ranking service runs on Cloudflare Workers with a D1 database. It associates records with a pseudonymous identifier derived from your Firebase UID; this does not make the records anonymous. Cloudflare also processes service and security information under its Privacy Policy. A result completed offline is queued on your device and may upload when you reconnect.

6. Optional platform cloud backups

Where enabled, Settings → Data and privacy offers separate Back up progress and Restore progress controls. Android uses Google Play Games saved games; iOS uses Game Center saved games with iCloud. These features require the relevant platform account and service availability, separately from Lumen's Firebase sign-in.

A backup includes your nickname, preset avatar, grade, preferences, practice sessions, answered-question records and Daily Spark completion, with a save timestamp. It excludes your custom local picture. Apple or Google stores the backup through its saved-game service. It is not sent to RevenueCat or stored as your Firebase Authentication profile.

Before replacement, the app previews the existing backup or local progress and asks you to confirm. Restoring replaces the described local progress and preferences; the app keeps a local recovery copy. Backups do not provide automatic merging between devices or transfer between Apple and Google services. See Google Play Games cloud-save information and the provider privacy policies above.

7. Questions, network use and permissions

Questions and fonts are bundled with Lumen. New questions arrive through app updates; there is no separate question-pack download or R2 content download in the current learning flow. Bundled content does not mean unlimited offline Pro access: subscription verification still applies.

Sign-in, rankings, platform backups, purchases, restoration and subscription refreshes use the network. Ranked attempts are downloaded from the ranking service. Opening store or support links uses the relevant external service.

Lumen has no advertising SDK, advertising tracking, or separate third-party usage-analytics or crash-reporting integration. Authentication, billing, ranking and platform-backup providers still process the service data described above. Lumen does not sell your learner profile or progress. We may disclose information when required by law or needed to handle a lawful request or protect users and the service.

Lumen does not need your contacts or precise device location. Where custom pictures are available, the device's file or photo picker supplies only the picture you choose. Online providers may process information in countries other than yours. Access checks and encrypted network connections help protect online features, but no service or storage method guarantees absolute security.

8. Deletion and retention

In Settings → Data and privacy → Delete account, confirm by signing in again with the same Google or Apple account. This removes your public leaderboard nickname/profile and deletes your Firebase sign-in account. The Apple deletion flow also requests revocation of Apple sign-in authorization. It does not delete your Google or Apple account.

Account deletion does not cancel your subscription or erase local progress. Cancel renewal separately through the store, and use Reset app to remove the device’s learning profile. Resetting or signing out does not delete the Firebase account.

Local profile information remains until you reset or remove it. Platform backups are separate: resetting, signing out, uninstalling and Firebase account deletion do not automatically remove the saved-game copy held by Apple or Google. Use the relevant platform's data controls or contact us for help identifying the available removal route.

Deleted leaderboard profiles are hidden from rankings immediately. Minimal pseudonymous profile and attempt records remain temporarily to enforce daily-attempt limits and prevent repeat scoring; the service's cleanup process removes deleted-profile records after a 42-day retention period. Removal depends on cleanup completing. Completed attempts are eligible for cleanup after 42 days; unfinished attempts for active accounts can remain longer to support later offline submission.

Firebase authentication records are subject to Firebase's deletion and backup-retention processes after account deletion. Deleting the Firebase account does not automatically delete RevenueCat customer records or Apple/Google transaction records. Those records can remain for subscription servicing and applicable accounting, security or legal requirements under the relevant provider's practices.

If you contact support, your email address, message and information you include are processed to handle the request. We do not specify a fixed retention deadline for support correspondence or provider billing records. You can request access, correction or deletion by email; we may need to verify account ownership and explain any records that cannot be deleted. See account-deletion instructions and Data controls.

9. Your requests and younger learners

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction or objection and to complain to a data-protection authority. Email us to exercise an applicable right. We may need to verify ownership and explain any information we must retain. Never send a password or sign-in code.

Lumen is intended for secondary-school learners and is not directed at children under 13. Children under 13 should not create an account or provide personal information through Lumen. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the situation. Lumen does not currently provide an age-verification flow.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its date when Lumen’s data practices change. For private questions, email glitchlabsio@gmail.com. Use Support for product help; do not post account details or other personal information in public GitHub issues.
