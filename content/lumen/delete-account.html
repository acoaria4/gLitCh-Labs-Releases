Delete your Lumen account

You can delete your Lumen sign-in account and public leaderboard profile in the app or request help by email. Store subscriptions, local learning progress and platform cloud backups require separate actions.

Delete in the app

  1. Connect to the internet and open Settings → Data and privacy in Lumen.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Read the deletion notice, then confirm by signing in again with the same Google or Apple account. If a different account is selected, the original account is not deleted.
  4. Wait for the account-deleted confirmation. If deletion fails, retry or contact support.

This removes the public leaderboard nickname/profile and deletes the Firebase sign-in account used by Lumen. For Apple sign-in, the app also requests revocation of the Apple authorization. Your Google or Apple account itself is not deleted.

Cancel renewal separately

Deleting your Lumen account does not cancel your subscription. If you want to stop renewal, use Manage subscription before deleting, or visit Apple subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions. Account deletion is not conditional on cancellation, but billing continues unless you cancel through the store.

Remove local progress separately

Account deletion leaves your local learning profile and practice history on this device. To clear them, open Settings → Data and privacy → Reset app → Reset and start again. This also clears the local recovery copy. Repeat on other devices where you have used Lumen. Resetting alone does not delete Firebase sign-in or server-side daily-attempt records.

Existing Google Play Games or Game Center/iCloud saved-game backups are separate from Firebase sign-in and are not automatically removed by account deletion or local reset. Use the relevant platform's data controls or contact us for help identifying the available removal route. Restoring an existing backup can bring its saved learning progress back to a device.

Cannot access the app or sign in?

Request account deletion by email ↗

Email glitchlabsio@gmail.com with the subject “Lumen account deletion request”. Include the Google or Apple sign-in email associated with Lumen, if known; Apple may use a private relay address. Tell us that you want your Lumen account deleted and whether you are also requesting deletion of associated support or billing-service data.

We may ask for information needed to verify ownership and clarify the request. Never send passwords, one-time sign-in codes or full payment-card details. Sending an email does not immediately delete data or cancel store billing.

What may remain

The deleted leaderboard profile is hidden from rankings immediately. Minimal pseudonymous records remain temporarily to enforce daily-attempt limits and prevent repeat scoring. The ranking service's cleanup process removes deleted-profile and associated attempt records after a 42-day retention period, subject to cleanup completion. Your nickname is not publicly visible during that period.

Firebase applies its deletion and backup-retention process. The app does not automatically delete RevenueCat customer records or Apple/Google transaction records when deleting Firebase sign-in. Provider records may remain for subscription servicing or accounting, security and legal requirements. Support correspondence is handled separately, with no fixed deletion deadline stated here.

Contact us about associated data; we can explain the available deletion steps and any retention limits. Read the Privacy Policy, Data controls and Terms of Service for details.
