Your data controls

Lumen keeps local learning progress, sign-in, ranked records, platform backups and store subscriptions separately. Here is what each control changes. Online controls depend on availability in your installed version.

Update your local profile

In Settings, use Profile, Learning and Appearance to change your nickname, preset avatar, grade and theme. In versions offering a custom local picture, you can replace or remove it. These changes do not change your Google or Apple profile.

When signed in and rankings are connected, your nickname, preset avatar, grade and streak are sent to the ranking service. Your leaderboard nickname, avatar, score and rank can be shown to other signed-in learners. Use a nickname without personal contact details. Custom local pictures are not uploaded to the leaderboard.

Reset local progress

Open Settings → Data and privacy → Reset app and confirm Reset and start again. This clears the device's nickname, picture if present, preferences, XP, streaks, practice history and local recovery copy, then returns you to onboarding. It does not delete bundled questions.

Resetting cannot be undone. It does not delete your Firebase account, leaderboard profile, server-side daily-attempt records, existing platform backup or store subscription. Clearing app or browser storage can also remove local information.

Sign out or delete your account

Use Settings → Account → Sign out to disconnect the current sign-in and clear current Pro access in the app while keeping the account and local progress. Settings → Data and privacy → Delete account requires signing in again with the same provider and deletes Firebase sign-in and the public leaderboard profile. Local progress, platform backups and store records are separate. Minimal pseudonymous ranked records remain temporarily for anti-abuse purposes. Follow the account-deletion instructions for details.

Cancel a subscription

Open Settings → Lumen Pro → Manage subscription or visit Apple subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions. Reset, sign-out, account deletion and uninstalling do not stop renewal. See Terms of Service for restoration and access limits.

Progress and backups

Where enabled, open Settings → Data and privacy and choose Back up progress or Restore progress. Android uses Google Play Games; iOS uses Game Center and iCloud. Sign in to the relevant platform account and review the preview before confirming.

Backing up replaces the platform's saved copy with the described local progress. Restoring replaces local progress and preferences with the selected backup; it keeps your current custom local picture and makes a local recovery copy. Backups exclude custom pictures. Signing in to Lumen or restoring a subscription does not perform this backup or restore for you, and backups do not transfer between Apple and Google services.

Local reset, Firebase account deletion and uninstalling do not delete a separate platform backup. Use the relevant platform's data controls or contact us for help identifying the available removal route.

Request help with your data

Read the Privacy Policy for account, billing and retention details. Request access, correction or deletion through glitchlabsio@gmail.com. Keep private account information out of public GitHub issues.
