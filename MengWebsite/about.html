About gLitCh Labs

gLitCh Labs is a small studio shipping focused, privacy-minded software. These are the commitments that shape every product decision.

  1. 01

    Clarity

    One job per surface. Copy stays precise. Interfaces stay quiet.

  2. 02

    Restraint

    Fewer features, stronger defaults. If it doesn’t earn its place, it doesn’t ship.

  3. 03

    Local-first

    Useful without an account when possible. Sync is optional — never the toll for basic utility.

  4. 04

    Privacy as fact

    Privacy is a product constraint, not a campaign slogan. Claims stay uninventable.

  5. 05

    Calm

    Motion and UI should feel unhurried. No hype. No noise for its own sake.

  6. 06

    Inevitability

    The outcome we’re after: “Of course that’s how it should work.”
