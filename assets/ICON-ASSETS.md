# Icon assets

Selected transparent PNGs are in `finalized-icons/`:

- `aura-solar-embrace-ivory.png`: active AURA website icon.
- `aura-solar-embrace-obsidian.png`: dark AURA alternative.
- `expenses-champagne-on-obsidian.png`: active Expenses website icon.

All three are native 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNGs. Their gold boundaries are preserved without clipping. Public site references, favicons, sharing metadata and the Expenses entry in Social Composer use this folder.

Draft boards and rejected variants stay under local `generations/` (gitignored). Lumen and studio artwork were not changed in this pass and remain at their existing paths.
