# Expenses icon alternatives

Three standalone concept assets generated with the built-in image generation tool, based on the approved Sculpted metal board. Original production assets are unchanged.

Each PNG is 1254 × 1254 pixels, RGBA, with transparent space outside the opaque squircle. Generation requested 2048 × 2048 but returned 1254 × 1254; these are native outputs, not upscaled. Each mark retains nine dots. Small geometry and framing differences exist between generated variants.

## Prompts

### champagne-on-obsidian

Create one standalone high resolution 2048 x 2048 PNG app icon from the attached approved Expenses concept board. Reproduce ONLY the LEFT large icon: champagne gold metal on near-black obsidian. Preserve its exact design, materials, subtle surface texture, bevel, restrained three-dimensional depth and lighting. One centered front-facing squircle filling approximately 94% of square canvas, fully visible with equal margins. Genuine transparent alpha background everywhere OUTSIDE the squircle, no white backdrop, no checkerboard drawn, no exterior cast shadow. The squircle surface remains opaque. Preserve the custom rounded E monogram with three arms ending in angled cuts and exactly NINE raised metal circular dots at lower right arranged in TWO staggered vertical columns: FOUR on left, FIVE on right. Crisp high fidelity edges and satin metal highlights. No purple hues. No text, captions, wordmark, border panel, secondary icon or layout. This is a reusable standalone final asset, not a presentation board. Render at 2048x2048 if supported.
