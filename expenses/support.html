Expenses by gLitCh Labs
App ID: com.glitchlabs.expenses
Last updated: 28 July 2026

Need help with Expenses? Use the options below. The app is local-first — your data stays on your device — so most issues can be fixed with a backup, reinstall, or a quick report.

1. Report a bug or ask a question

Open an issue on GitHub (preferred): github.com/acoaria4/expenses-by-glitchlabs/issues

Include your device model, OS version, and app version (Settings → About) when possible.

2. Common fixes

  • Lost data after reinstall — restore from a JSON backup in Settings if you exported one earlier.
  • Keyboard covering a field — dismiss and refocus the field, or update to the latest version from the store / TestFlight.
  • Theme or preferences reset — confirm you are on the same device; data is not synced across phones.

3. Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for what the app stores and what it does not collect.
