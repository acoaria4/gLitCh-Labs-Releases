Privacy Policy

Expenses by gLitCh Labs
App ID: com.glitchlabs.expenses
Last updated: 15 July 2026

Expenses is a local-first personal and shared expense tracker. We designed it so your financial data stays on your device. This policy explains what the app stores, what it does not collect, and how backups work.

1. Data we store

Expenses stores the following on your device only:

  • Profile details you enter (such as display name)
  • Personal expenses, categories, budgets, and settings
  • Shared groups, members, shared expenses, and settlements
  • Activity history generated from your actions in the app
  • Theme, currency, and backup preferences

On Android and iOS this data is stored using on-device app storage (Capacitor Preferences). On the web version it is stored in your browser (IndexedDB). Nothing in the above list is uploaded to gLitCh Labs servers, because the app has no account backend.

2. Data we do not collect

  • No account sign-up or login
  • No cloud sync of expenses (unless a future version you opt into adds it)
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the current app
  • No selling or sharing of personal financial data with third parties

3. Backups you choose to make

You may export a JSON backup or enable optional automatic local backups. Those files are created on your device (or downloaded by you). We do not receive them unless you choose to send a file to someone (for example by email or cloud drive of your own).

4. Permissions and device features

The app may use standard platform features needed to run as a mobile app (such as storage for preferences, status bar / splash screen presentation). It does not require access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or location for core expense tracking.

5. Children

Expenses is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app to store information about children if you do not have authority to do so.

6. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way (for example if a future release adds optional cloud sync), we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
