Your data controls

AURA stores guest and account horoscopes separately on your device. Account cloud backup, hosted calculation caches and exported files are separate copies. These controls help you manage them; read the Privacy Policy for the full explanation.

Export or import horoscopes

Open the Privacy panel and choose the export action to save the horoscopes available in your current guest or account view. Settings also offers a file-import action. Importing places the charts into the currently selected guest or account storage. Backup files contain names, birth details and chart information, so store and share them carefully.

Manage cloud backup

Where cloud backup is available, sign in to your AURA account and use Sync cloud backup in Settings. Check that backup completes before relying on another device to have the latest chart. Importing this device's guest charts into your account is a separate action; signing in does not itself move them.

Delete a saved horoscope from the active guest or account view to remove that local record. For a backed-up account chart, reconnect and allow synchronization to send the deletion to the service. Devices that have not synchronized can still hold older copies.

Clear the local cache

The Privacy panel's cache-clearing action removes the legacy local chart cache. It does not delete all saved horoscopes, cloud backups or the hosted service's prediction cache. Use saved-chart deletion or contact support for those separate records.

Choose optional features

Where available in your version, Privacy settings control online geocoding and tradition-corpus citations. Disabling online geocoding limits location lookup to the available local place database. For daily reminders, use Settings → Notifications, or change AURA's notification permission in your device settings.

Delete your AURA account

While signed in, open Settings and choose Delete account, then read and confirm the deletion prompt. This requests deletion of the AURA sign-in account and its cloud backups and clears that account's saved data from the current device. Guest charts remain. Your Google or Apple account is not deleted.

If you cannot sign in or cannot access the deletion control, email glitchlabsio@gmail.com with the subject “AURA account deletion request”. Include the sign-in email, if known; Apple may have supplied a private relay address. We may need to verify account ownership. Never send a password or sign-in code.

Reset this device

Reset AURA on this device in Settings clears local horoscopes, unsynced edits, preferences and caches and signs you out on that device. Export local-only charts first. This does not delete your online account, cloud backup, exported files or store purchase records. Uninstalling is not an account-deletion request.

Request deletion of other information

Hosted prediction caches, support messages and payment records are separate from the account and local reset controls. Ask us for help identifying and deleting information associated with you. Some provider records may need to remain for transactions, security or legal obligations; we will explain any applicable limits.

Email a data request ↗

Send private requests by email. Keep birth details, reports and account information out of public GitHub issues.
