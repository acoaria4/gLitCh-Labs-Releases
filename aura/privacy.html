AURA by gLitCh Labs
App ID: com.glitchlabs.aura
Last updated: 6 November 2026

AURA provides Vedic astrology charts, calendar information, compatibility comparisons and readings. This policy explains the information used by the app, optional sign-in and chart backups, report purchases, and your data controls. AURA is free to use; a detailed prediction report is a separate ₹999 one-time purchase, not a subscription. See our Terms of Service for purchase terms.

1. Who is responsible for your information

AURA is published and operated by gLitCh Labs. For a privacy question or request, email glitchlabsio@gmail.com. Do not post birth details, account information or reports in a public GitHub issue.

2. Information you enter and information stored on your device

AURA uses the birth date, birth time and birthplace you enter to calculate a chart and related interpretations. Saved horoscopes can contain a name or label, birth details, calculated chart information and readings. Compatibility comparisons use information from the two charts you choose.

Saved horoscopes are stored on your device. Guest horoscopes and each signed-in account use separate local storage. The app also stores preferences such as appearance, language, privacy choices, your selected personal horoscope and optional reminder settings. Sign-in sessions are persisted using the platform's app storage so you can remain signed in.

You can export the horoscopes available in your current guest or account view. Exported files contain personal information; keep them private. Importing a backup places its horoscope information into the currently selected guest or account storage.

3. Chart calculations and hosted services

Supported app versions can calculate basic charts using an on-device native calculation engine. Where a feature uses the hosted AURA API, the birth inputs, chart information, compatibility inputs or place-search query needed for that feature are sent to our service, currently hosted on Fly.io. Reading and report availability depends on the installed release.

The hosted service can store prediction results and the birth inputs used to calculate them in a server-side cache. This calculation cache is separate from your saved horoscopes and cloud backup. The app sends an installation/client identifier and app platform, version and build information with relevant requests. Network and hosting services also process connection information, such as IP addresses and request information, to deliver and protect the service.

The public day and month calendar does not require a personal birth chart. Its default place configuration is Chennai / Tamil Nadu. Self-hosted deployments can process data on the operator's own computer or server; any external services they enable remain subject to their own settings.

4. Optional Google or Apple sign-in and cloud backup

You can use guest features without creating an AURA account. Where sign-in is available, AURA uses Supabase Authentication with Google or Apple. Supabase processes a user identifier, provider identity, and available account information such as email address, display name and profile information supplied by the provider. Apple may provide a private relay email address. AURA does not receive your Google or Apple password.

Where cloud backup is enabled for your signed-in account, saved horoscope names and chart data are sent through the AURA service to an account-scoped database in Supabase. Record identifiers, revision numbers and update/deletion information support synchronization between your devices. Deletion markers can remain to prevent a deleted chart from returning during synchronization.

Guest charts are not automatically assigned to a newly signed-in account. The app provides a separate import option to copy guest charts into that account. Signing out switches the active account view; it does not itself erase local account charts or the cloud copy. Backup requires connectivity and a successful sync. A local save is not proof that a cloud backup has completed.

See Supabase's Privacy Policy, Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy for information about those providers.

5. Optional place lookup, citations and reminders

Place search uses the available place database. If online geocoding is enabled and a location needs an online lookup, the place query may be sent to the Nominatim geocoding service and its result cached. A place lookup does not require sending the person's name or full horoscope to Nominatim. Where your version offers the control, disabling online geocoding limits lookup to the available local database. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

Optional tradition-corpus citations use a local classical-text index on the calculation service. Initial setup may download an embedding model. This does not make your hosted chart calculations local to your phone.

Native app versions offer an optional daily reminder. Enabling it requests notification permission and schedules a local notification on your device. The reminder preference and chosen time are stored locally. Disable the reminder in AURA's Settings or revoke notification permission in your device settings.

6. Report purchases and support

If you buy a detailed prediction report, the payment provider identified in the purchase flow processes payment. We may process an order reference, product/report identifier, purchase status, purchase time and the account or installation identifier needed to give you access to the report. The birth inputs and chart information required to create the report are processed separately from payment-card information. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Where a release uses Apple or Google purchasing and RevenueCat purchase verification, those services process transaction and technical information needed to verify and restore access. An AURA account identifier can associate the purchase with your account. This is purchase verification, not an AURA subscription. See the purchasing provider's notice and, where used, RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

If you email support, we process your email address, message and information you include to answer your request. Share only the details needed for the issue. Never send passwords, sign-in codes or full payment-card details.

7. How information is used and shared

We use the information described above to provide charts and reports, save and synchronize horoscopes you choose to back up, authenticate accounts, verify purchases, respond to support requests and protect the service. Service providers receive the information needed for those functions. They may process it in countries other than where you live, under their applicable service and data-protection arrangements.

AURA has no advertising SDK or advertising tracking, and we do not sell birth or chart information to data brokers. Core astrology features do not require contacts, microphone or camera access. Exporting or sharing a file uses the destination you choose. External store, support and website links are handled by the relevant external service.

8. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local charts and preferences remain until you remove them, reset the app or clear its storage. Removing a chart from a signed-in account creates a deletion that is sent to cloud backup when synchronization succeeds. Devices that have not yet synchronized can still contain older local copies. Exported files are outside the app's deletion controls.

In Settings, Delete account requests removal of the AURA sign-in account and its cloud-backed horoscopes, then clears that account's horoscope data from the current device. Guest charts remain. Deleting AURA's account does not delete your Google or Apple account. Reset AURA on this device is a separate action that clears local horoscopes, preferences, caches and sign-in on that device; it does not delete your online account or cloud backups.

The Privacy panel's cache-clearing control removes the app's legacy local chart cache; it does not erase the hosted prediction cache. Deleting an account or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete hosted calculation caches, support correspondence or payment-provider records. Provider backups and records needed for security, transactions or legal obligations may also remain under the relevant provider's processes.

We do not promise a fixed deletion deadline for every record. You can ask us for access, correction, export or deletion of information associated with you at glitchlabsio@gmail.com. Tell us which account or installation the request concerns; we may need to verify ownership and identify the relevant records. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent for optional features, or complain to a data-protection authority. See Data controls for the available in-app actions.

9. Children

AURA is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit a child's birth details unless you are a parent or guardian with authority to do so. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authority so we can review the situation.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this policy and the date above when AURA's data practices change. For private questions, contact gLitCh Labs at glitchlabsio@gmail.com.
